Account and profile information

KETUA stores the account identifiers, profile details, photos, interests and preferences you submit. Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Backend phone registration uses a password and does not currently verify ownership of a phone number. An account login or paid plan does not grant an identity-verification badge.

Location and discovery

When you grant location access, the app can submit a device location and its accuracy to support discovery. Your public profile shows an approximate distance where suitable location data is available. Exact coordinates are not included in public profile responses. Permissions can be changed in your device settings.

Messages, content and interactions

KETUA processes messages, uploads, likes, matches, gifts, reports and blocking settings to provide the service and investigate abuse. Profile and community content is visible according to the relevant feature and account settings. Paid content requires a server-verified unlock. Messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

Payments

Payment services process checkout information. KETUA records orders, verified payment status, account balances, tickets and purchased access. The current mobile-money checkout uses Pesapal. Never send a mobile-money PIN through chat or enter it into a KETUA text field. Store purchases require the relevant store configuration.

Saved information on your device

The app saves profile settings and caches images to improve navigation. Recent chat and community snapshots are stored using the device's secure storage, scoped to your account and removed on logout. These snapshots expire after one day. Financial actions and permissions continue to be checked by the server.
