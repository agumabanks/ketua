# KETUA: launch quality and 90-day priorities

The strongest initial promise is: **discover a personality, make a connection, and plan a real date within your budget.** Content supports discovery; creator earnings reward quality. Market leadership in a few months is a goal, not a guaranteed outcome. Establish a dependable local experience before expanding the catalog and geography.

## Immediate engineering work — accelerated

The first fast-track pass is implemented. See [the acceptance report](FAST-TRACK-QUALITY-PASS-2026-10-11.md) for actual changes and evidence. Continue prioritizing reliable navigation, privacy, correct booking states and payment integrity immediately; the day ranges below describe product learning and supply growth, not a delay in engineering work.

## Before public paid launch

Close the real launch gates in the market audit: verified payments/reversals and reconciliation, published policies/support details, reviewed creator rates, actual listings and fulfillment, live/store provider configuration where enabled, reproducible release builds and physical-device testing. Unavailable functions should have honest availability states rather than optimistic purchase buttons.

Audit spacing systematically: consistent page gutters; 8/12/16/24 spacing steps; 48dp touch targets; clear section boundaries; flexible rows; scrollable content above fixed actions; system/keyboard safe areas. Check 320dp screens and 200% text, not just the OPPO. Notifications need readable messages, timestamps and meaningful actions. Listings need clear unit prices, availability, inclusions, cancellation rules and confirmation status.

## First 30 days: trust, speed and useful local supply

- Focus the controlled launch on one area, initially Kampala. Recruit real consenting members, approved creators and dependable venues; clearly separate QA profiles from public discovery. Population density and useful inventory matter more than a long menu of empty modules.
- Finish reliable Google/phone account onboarding, ownership/recovery, photo editing and fresh approximate location. Core reporting, blocking and identity verification should be accessible without buying Gold. Paid membership must not masquerade as identity verification.
- Use account-scoped cached views, image prefetch and background updates. Preserve scroll/filter/draft state and reconcile optimistic actions. Server-check money, inventory and entitlements; do not present stale wallet data as authoritative.
- Review all key journeys with real users: first account, first like, mutual match, message, gift, reservation and earner application. Track where users stop or misunderstand.

## Days 31–60: a distinctive connection-to-date experience

- Add a shared date planner inside chat: suggest budget, area and available times; choose from approved venues; invite the match; show confirmed/pending/cancelled status to both consenting participants. Make the venue confirmation and refund rules explicit.
- Improve discovery with clear relationship goals, interests and personality prompts. Explain recommendations in ordinary language, provide feedback and respect strict preferences, blocks and privacy. Content can introduce someone without forcing endless video scrolling.
- Upgrade notifications with unread state, grouped events, quiet hours and specific destinations: match to chat, reservation to booking, gift to receipt, payout to history. Avoid repetitive streak or marketing alerts.
- Give creators a clear net-earnings dashboard: gross value, platform share, pending hold, released amount, requested payout, paid amount and support/dispute path.

## Days 61–90: grow only what works

- Add consent-based referrals and local venue/creator partnerships. Avoid fake scarcity, fabricated activity or paid reviews.
- Expand experiences based on fulfilled demand. Introduce creator subscriptions/live content only after moderation, billing and delivery are working.
- Use cohort retention, meaningful conversations, confirmed-and-fulfilled reservations, successful payments, support resolution and net contribution margin to choose expansion. Installs alone are not proof of product quality.

## Coin simplicity proposal — not yet applied

Recommended rule: **1 paid coin = UGX 1 of in-app spending value**. Present both the gift price and UGX amount; example: `Rose · 1,000 coins (UGX 1,000)`. This is an example price, not a configured catalog price.

- UGX 10,000 buys 10,000 paid coins before any separately disclosed checkout fee. If packs have bonuses/discounts, state them; do not imply identical paid backing for free bonus coins.
- Creator withdrawal value remains the admin-configured share of eligible paid value. Show net UGX earnings directly. A user's coin wallet is not an unrestricted cash-withdrawal account.
- Show promotional/test coins separately as non-withdrawable. Existing balances and gifts need a reviewed migration; never silently change accumulated value.
- Keep Uganda-facing spending in UGX. USD checkout must display the actual conversion/fees and coin amount before confirmation; no invented fixed exchange rate.
- Reconcile platform/provider fees and creator shares so payouts cannot exceed backing. Snapshot gift prices, conversion and share when charged/released; subsequent admin edits affect future transactions according to published rules.

Store distribution affects the payment route for digital currency, content and subscriptions. Review [Google Play Payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) and [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase). Venue stays and physical services require their applicable booking/payment flow. Do not assume renaming coins makes store or financial requirements disappear.

## Release measurements

Use real measured baselines, then agree numerical launch thresholds: crash-free sessions, cold/warm startup and navigation latency on affordable phones; booking/payment success and reconciliation exceptions; seven-/thirty-day retention; first meaningful conversation; fulfillment/cancellations; reports handled and payout settlement. Financial acceptance criteria include zero unexplained ledger differences and no double debit/reservation in replay/concurrency tests. Run small controlled cohorts before broad marketing.
