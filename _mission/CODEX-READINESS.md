## Commerce now implemented

- Free: 50 likes per server-defined session, no rewinds. Session resets after 30 minutes without a swipe or after 24 hours.
- Gold: unlimited likes, five rewinds per day, four Super Likes per week, incoming Likes, worldwide country discovery, hide-age/distance, incognito and optional ad hiding. Pro: unlimited rewinds plus its actual configured priority benefit. Blocks and consent still apply; payment does not grant verification or guarantee matches.
- Gold UGX: 7 days **10,000 total**; 30 days **37,000 total**, displayed as **9,250/week**; 90 days **36,000 total**, displayed as **3,000/week**. Weekly comparison explicitly uses four weeks for the monthly package and twelve for the three-month package. The full one-time charge and actual access period remain visible. All prices/rules are admin editable. Pro/USD prices remain unset.
- Super Like packs: five for **UGX 10,000**, fifteen for **UGX 15,000**. Included/purchased inventory is consumed by the server. Admin can create coin packs and adjust all pack prices/quantities; no gift-coin exchange rate was invented.
- Paid event/live-event tickets with transactional capacity checks; private paid image/video unlocks and pending creator-sale records; labeled admin-sponsored Swipe/community cards with entitlement-aware ad hiding. Clearly labeled preview events remain unbookable; no fictitious real event or paid creator content was published.
- UGX mobile-money account loading adapts Finance's Pesapal integration. Credentials remain in KETUA's private server directory. Provider authentication and KETUA IPN registration passed. Provider callbacks are verified server-to-server against the exact order, amount and currency. Ledger entries, grants and reversals are idempotent; spent reversed funds create a debt/freeze instead of free money. Account funds and gift coins remain separate.
- Account-funded purchases use actual balance, server prices and confirmation. Repeated keys cannot charge twice or change their meaning. Pending payment can resume after reopening the app. No production wallet, paid entitlement or badge was fabricated.
